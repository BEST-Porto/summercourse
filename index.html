About BEST Porto

Founded in 1996, BEST Porto is a Local BEST Group based in the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto, but also with representation in the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Porto.

Currently, BEST Porto has about 80 active members and organizes local and international events, reaching about 11.000 students. As part of this community, we strive to create opportunities for personal and professional growth through complementary education, career support, and cultural exchange.

About BEST Symposium on Education

The BEST Symposium on Education (BSE) is an event that creates a platform for students, professors, researchers, and industry representatives to discuss educational matters while aiming to improve higher education in Europe. This year's topic is Engineering Education in the Age of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Digital Transformation.

All the knowledge acquired during this Symposium will be presented at leading European conferences, raising new opportunities for collaboration and inspiring innovative research directions across the field.

Learn More

What is this Symposium about?

This event brings together motivated experts and students from all over Europe to rethink engineering education in an AI-driven world.

Through inspiring discussions, interactive workshops, and collaborative activities, participants will explore how AI is reshaping learning environments and the skills engineers need for the future.

Location

As usual, the course will mostly occur in Porto, the northern city at the mouth of Douro River. It is the second largest metropolitan area in the country and has won numerous awards as a travel destination, such as being elected the World’s Leading City Break Destination 2020.

The vibrant city of Porto is known as the ‘Cidade Invicta’ since it stands out for its resilient and fascinating history. From the stunning riverside views to the charming old houses with their bright, tiled facades, every corner of the city invites you to explore and fall in love with its unique atmosphere. You can also immerse yourself in it while enjoying the world-famous Port wine!

Porto never fails to enchant every visitor, and this course promises to reveal its authentic charm to you!

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

This course is intended for higher education students from courses related to technology (Engineering or Sciences), curious about how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way future engineers learn, think, and create.

What are the selection criteria?

The participants will be selected through a motivational letter, in which they show certain information about themselves (such as originality, interest in the topic given and disposition to have fun and meet new people) and also through answering some questions in their motivational letter.

How much is the fee to attend and what does it cover?

The participants will have to pay a fee with a maximum value of 80€. It covers accommodation, transportation and meals (three meals per day, at least one of them hot) throughout the entire course. The arrival and departure costs are the responsibility of each participant.
